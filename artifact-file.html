Built for real estate

Your chart of accounts should know what a unit is.

Most accounting software treats a project as a tag. NeoAccounting treats it as structure: every organization can carry multiple legal entities, each legal entity its own projects, each project its buildings, each building its units — and every invoice, bill, and journal line can be traced straight down to one.

That's also where revenue recognition lives: tag an invoice line with a service period and NeoAccounting spreads the income across it automatically, with a Deferred Revenue report that always matches the ledger.
